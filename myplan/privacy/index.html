MyPlan Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

MyPlan ("the app") is an all-in-one health tracker with an AI assistant, operated by Scoped (scopedagency.com) ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The short version: your data is used to run the app for you — we don't sell it and we don't use it for advertising.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Health and fitness information you provide — meals and nutrition estimates, workouts, water intake, body metrics (such as weight, height, and age), goals, tasks, habit and reading streaks, and notes or "memories" you or the assistant save.
  • Messages and uploads — your conversations with the AI assistant and any photos or documents you attach (for example, meal photos or lab results you choose to share).

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or browsing history, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

How your information is used

  • To provide the app's features: logging, plans, streaks, reminders, and your history.
  • To generate AI responses: your messages, relevant saved memories, and recent logs are sent to our backend, which uses third-party AI model providers to produce replies and workout plans. These providers process the content to generate the response and are not permitted to use it for advertising.
  • To send account emails: sign-up confirmation and password resets.

Where it lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage) and processed by our API hosted on Vercel. Data is protected in transit with TLS, and access is scoped to your account through row-level security.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with third parties except the service providers named above, which process it only on our behalf, and where required by law.

Apple Reminders and notifications

If you enable the optional Reminders integration or meal check-ins, task titles are mirrored into your device's Reminders app and local notifications are scheduled on your device. These features are off by default and can be turned off anytime in Settings.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account anytime in the app (Settings → Delete account), which permanently deletes your account and everything in it — logs, memories, plans, conversations, and uploaded files. This cannot be undone.

Children

MyPlan is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

MyPlan provides general wellness information and AI-generated suggestions. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified health provider about medical questions.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and the date above.
